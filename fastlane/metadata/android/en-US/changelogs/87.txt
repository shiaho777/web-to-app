Added
- Fullscreen apps can choose how the display cutout is handled.
- Pages can read display cutout insets from CSS variables.
- Multi-web drawer layouts can use a floating drawer button.
- Multi-web cards hide the title bar while scrolling up.

Improved
- Cross-origin requests stay on the WebView stack, so large downloads stream again.
- Multi-web sites recover after Android kills the renderer in the background.
- Multi-web layouts paint the configured status bar overlay.
- Source exports can be restored from Data Backup > Import Data.
- Installing through QQ and vendor app stores no longer reports a missing certificate.

Fixed
- A splash video survives Save after Preview.
- Long category lists and edge-of-screen long-press menus stay reachable.
- About opens without a first-frame stall.
