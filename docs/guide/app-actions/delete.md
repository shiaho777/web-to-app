# Delete

Removes an app. There are two ways:

- **Action menu** — tap ⋮ on the app card, then **Delete**.
- **Swipe** — swipe the card left to reveal the delete affordance.

## How it works

Either path asks for **confirmation** before deleting. Confirm to remove the app permanently from [My Apps](/guide/main-screen/my-apps).

## Notes

- Deleting removes the app's definition from the builder. APKs you already built remain in the [File Manager](/guide/more-features/file-manager) until you clear them.
- To organize without deleting, use [Move to Category](/guide/app-actions/move-to-category).
