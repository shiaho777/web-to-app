# App Categories

The tab row beneath the top bar on [My Apps](/guide/main-screen/my-apps) organizes your apps into categories.

## Using categories

- **Select** a tab to filter the [app list](/guide/main-screen/app-list) to that category.
- **Add** a category with the ＋ action — give it a **name** and an **icon**.
- **Edit** an existing category (rename or change its icon).
- **Delete** a category you no longer need.

## Moving apps between categories

An app's category is changed from its action menu: tap ⋮ on the app card, then **Move to Category**, and pick the destination. See [Move to Category](/guide/app-actions/move-to-category).

Categories are purely organizational — they don't affect how an app builds or runs.
